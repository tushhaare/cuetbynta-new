❓ Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Start with the essentials, then use the subject resources above to plan your next step.

What is CUET UG?

CUET UG is an entrance examination used by participating universities for undergraduate admissions. Check each university’s current admission information for course-specific requirements.

Where can I find the syllabus?

Open the CUET syllabus directory above and choose the language, domain subject or aptitude test you need.

How should I practise?

Use the free mock tests to practise by subject, then review your answers and revisit topics that need more work.

Where can I check current updates?

Visit the Current Affairs page for the latest feed, and confirm exam notices with the official NTA and university sources.
