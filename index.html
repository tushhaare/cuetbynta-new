📁 What we are

One Idea, One Community — 2023.

CUETbyNTA started in 2023 with a simple goal: to make CUET and higher-education information easier for students to understand and access. What began as a student-led initiative has grown into an educational community helping aspirants navigate examinations, admissions, university choices, and everything in between. From updates and resources to guidance and mentorship, the focus has always been on making the student journey a little clearer.

Who's actually behind it

A student-led team working alongside mentors, educators, and volunteers who understand the questions and challenges students face. From creating educational resources and sharing important updates to conducting webinars, doubt-solving sessions, mentorship programmes, and admission guidance, the people behind CUETbyNTA are focused on one thing: making useful information reach students when they need it.

What changed

CUETbyNTA started around CUET and admission-related information, but the initiative has grown beyond just examination updates. Today, it brings together academic resources, student guidance, mentorship, peer learning, and educational outreach across digital platforms. The goal remains the same: reduce confusion, simplify information, and help students make more informed decisions about their education.
