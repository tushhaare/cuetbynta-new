CUET UG 2027 Exam Date: Expected Timeline and Official Updates

Looking for the CUET UG 2027 exam date? NTA has not published the 2027 information bulletin or exam schedule on the official CUET portal as of 25 October 2026. Here is what is confirmed, what is only an estimate, and where to check first.

By CUETbyNTA EditorialUpdated 25 October 2026Free student guide
2027 status: No official CUET UG 2027 exam date has been announced as of 25 October 2026. Treat every 2027 date shown below as a planning estimate, not an NTA announcement.

When could CUET UG 2027 take place?

The safest answer is that the date is not known yet. The latest available reference is the CUET UG 2026 information bulletin. It opened the application window from 3 to 30 January 2026 and initially listed the exam for 11 to 31 May 2026 as tentative. NTA notices later included schedule changes and re-scheduled candidates, so even a published timetable can be revised.

For planning only, students can prepare for a winter or early-2027 application announcement and an exam period around late spring, if the next cycle broadly follows the 2026 pattern. That is an inference from one recent cycle, not a prediction from NTA. Do not book travel, pay an agent, or make a deadline decision using an unofficial expected date.

How to track the real CUET 2027 schedule

  1. Bookmark the official CUET UG website.
  2. Read the 2027 information bulletin before relying on dates, paper choices, eligibility or fees.
  3. Check the NTA documents and public notices for extensions, correction windows, city slips, admit cards and rescheduling.
  4. Use the application and result links published by NTA; avoid look-alike sites and social posts without an official notice.

What should you do while waiting?

Start with your target university and course requirements. Make a shortlist of programmes, check their required CUET papers, and build a study plan from the current official subject syllabi. When NTA publishes the 2027 bulletin, compare every subject, timing and marking rule with your plan and revise it.

Dates can move, but the work you do now does not have to: strengthen Class 12 concepts, practise timed questions, and keep your documents and login email accessible. We will update this guide when an official 2027 notice appears.
