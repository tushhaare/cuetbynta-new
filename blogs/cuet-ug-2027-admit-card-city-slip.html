Admit card

CUET UG 2027 Admit Card and Exam City Slip: How to Check

NTA has not issued CUET UG 2027 city-slip or admit-card dates. When those documents are released, use the official portal and check every exam detail carefully. This guide explains what each document is for.

By CUETbyNTA EditorialUpdated 25 October 2026Free student guide
2027 status: City-slip and admit-card release dates are not announced. The order and timing will be specified by NTA in the 2027 notices.

City intimation slip vs. admit card

A city intimation notice tells a candidate the examination city so they can plan travel. It is not a substitute for the admit card and may not contain the final centre address or all reporting instructions. The admit card is the document candidates are instructed to bring to the exam centre; it contains the details and rules for the assigned paper, date, shift and centre.

Use the terms carefully: don’t book non-refundable travel based only on a social-media screenshot or assume the city slip is your entry document. Read the individual NTA notice, because the exam authority’s instructions take precedence.

Where to download the CUET UG admit card

Start from the official CUET UG portal and follow the candidate activity link in the relevant 2027 notice. Use only the login method described by NTA. The 2026 cycle had separate notices for examination city information and admit cards, with later rescheduling notices for affected candidates.

Check these details as soon as it is available

  • Your name and application details.
  • Subject or test paper, date, shift and reporting time.
  • Examination centre name and complete address on the admit card.
  • Photograph, signature and any instructions for permitted documents.
  • Current instructions about permitted items, dress, reporting and PwBD arrangements.

If you find a mismatch, use the helpdesk or procedure in the NTA notice promptly. Save a copy offline and keep a printed copy if the instructions require one. Bring only the identification and items permitted for your exam session.

What if the date or centre changes?

Check the official notice feed again shortly before travelling. In 2026, NTA published rescheduling notices for some candidates, illustrating why candidates should follow new official announcements rather than rely on an old screenshot. If you are named in a notice, follow its specific instructions and download any revised document.

After the 2027 city slip or admit card is released, this guide should be updated with the confirmed link and date. Until then, the 2026 notices are examples, not a 2027 schedule.
