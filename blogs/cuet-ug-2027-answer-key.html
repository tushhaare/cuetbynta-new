CUET UG 2027 Answer Key: Response Sheet, Challenge and Final Key

The CUET UG 2027 answer-key schedule has not been announced. This guide explains the usual stages to watch for, using NTA’s published 2026 process as a reference—not a guarantee.

By CUETbyNTA EditorialUpdated 25 October 2026Free student guide
2027 status: NTA has not announced when CUET UG 2027 provisional keys, recorded responses or final keys will be released.

What is a provisional CUET answer key?

A provisional key is NTA’s initial set of answers for the examination. Candidates may also be given access to recorded responses or question papers, subject to the notice for that cycle. These materials let you compare your responses with the key and identify a question you believe needs review.

How does the answer-key challenge work?

When the challenge window opens, read the NTA notice before submitting anything. It explains which questions can be challenged, the online submission steps, the evidence format and any fee or deadline. Use clear supporting evidence from an authoritative source. Save the submitted challenge details and payment acknowledgement if applicable.

Do not assume a challenge is accepted because it was submitted. NTA reviews challenges and publishes a final answer key or related notice. The exact process, charge and window length can change; follow the 2027 notice rather than copying a previous cycle’s deadline or fee.

How to estimate your score carefully

Use only the marking scheme published for your year and paper. In the 2026 bulletin, the stated scheme was +5 for a correct response and −1 for an incorrect response. Your 2027 marking may differ. Also, the score shown by a coaching answer key is an estimate and is not an official result. Do not confuse a raw self-calculation with the score or normalized score NTA may later report.

Where to find the official answer key

Start at the CUET UG official website and open the latest notice or document. The official 2026 documents archive shows that provisional question-response material and final answer-key notices are separate items. Keep checking the year and title before opening an old PDF.

Once NTA announces the 2027 answer-key window, update your calendar, gather question-specific evidence and submit through the official system before the deadline. Use this guide as a process checklist only; the official notice is authoritative.
