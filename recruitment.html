Open roles

Three ways to support students

We are accepting applications from freshers in the CUET UG 2026 batch. Applicants must have cleared CUET UG 2026 and be bona-fide students of the University of Delhi (DU). Please apply only if you understand and are comfortable with the unpaid, 10-month commitment.

People & guidance

Mentor & Community Manager

Help students feel heard, find reliable information and stay connected to a supportive learning community.

  • Respond to common student questions with care
  • Coordinate mentors, discussions and community activities
  • Escalate uncertain or time-sensitive exam information
Content & outreach

Social Media Executive

Turn useful CUET information into clear, accurate and engaging content for students.

  • Plan posts and short-form content
  • Write captions and adapt content to each platform
  • Check accuracy, tone and basic performance
Planning & delivery

Operations Associate

Keep projects organized so resources, events and student support arrive on time.

  • Track tasks, owners and deadlines
  • Coordinate across volunteers and mentors
  • Document processes and flag delivery risks early
What’s in it for you?

Make a difference and grow with the community.

  • A chance to make a real difference in the lives of thousands of students.
  • Certification for your hard work and contributions.
  • Letters of Recommendation (LOR) to support your future endeavors.

How selection works

After you submit the application, our team will review it and share next steps using the contact details you provide. Selection is at the discretion of CUETbyNTA’s admins; submitting the form does not guarantee selection.

Apply by 5 November 2026 at 11:59 AM IST.

Application

Complete your application.

The form asks for your contact and student details, post preference, CUET score, course and college, competitive-exam plans, availability, and your vision for CUETbyNTA. You’ll also need to upload your CUET scorecard and proof of DU student status (ID card or CSAS fee receipt showing your name). Tally accepts files up to 10 MB each on the free plan.

What to have ready

  • Your CUET score and scorecard.
  • Your current course and DU college, plus proof of student status.
  • The post you’re applying for and your weekly availability.
  • Whether you can commit to the full 10-month unpaid internship.
  • Your plans for other competitive exams and your vision for CUETbyNTA.

Please ensure your application information is accurate. The declaration confirms that you cannot edit it later and agree that CUETbyNTA representatives may call or text you for further enquiries.
